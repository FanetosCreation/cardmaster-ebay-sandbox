Card Master — eBay Sandbox Privacy Notice

This application is currently used for development and testing with the eBay Sandbox environment.

Card Master may access basic account identity, seller business policies, and inventory-location information authorized through eBay OAuth.

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are not stored on this website. This website does not receive or process the user's eBay login credentials.

This Sandbox integration does not publish, modify, or sell items on the Production eBay marketplace.
